# AI Lead Tracker

Captures leads from Instagram (or any social media) DMs/website webhook, uses AI to score them (Hot/Warm/Cold), and stores them in Google Sheets.

## How it works
1. Instagram DM arrives via webhook
2. AI scores the lead
3. Google Sheets stores the lead
4. Telegram alerts on hot leads

## Tech
n8n, Google Gemini, Google Sheets, Telegram
