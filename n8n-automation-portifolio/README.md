# n8n Automation Portfolio

High school student building automations with n8n, AI, and APIs.

## Projects

### 🔥 AI Lead Tracker
Captures leads from Instagram (or any social media) DMs/, uses AI to score them (Hot/Warm/Cold), stores in Google Sheets, and alerts on hot leads.

**Tech:** n8n, Google Gemini, Google Sheets, Telegram


### 🌐 Multi-Source Lead Aggregator
Unified pipeline for leads from Instagram + Discord + Website. AI scores, logs, and alerts.

**Tech:** n8n, Google Gemini, Google Sheets, Discord


### 📧 Internal Email Categorizer
Categorizes incoming emails (urgent, order, question, lead, spam), logs to Google Sheets, creates tasks for urgent ones, and alerts the team.

**Tech:** n8n, Gmail, Google Gemini, Google Sheets, Google Tasks, Telegram


### 🌤️ Telegram Weather Bot
Fetches live weather data and sends daily reports to Telegram.

**Tech:** n8n, OpenWeather API, Telegram
